Analysis Method

The Web Audio API receives the audio signal. AnalyserNode performs FFT analysis and provides the magnitude of frequency components. Consecutive FFT frames are displayed from left to right to form the spectrogram.

Frequency Validation

A known-frequency sine wave can be used to verify the frequency calibration. An A4 tone has a fundamental frequency of approximately 440 Hz. Click the validation button to zoom the frequency axis to 0–2 kHz.

Axis & Scale

The horizontal axis represents time in seconds. The vertical axis represents frequency in Hertz (Hz). The frequency axis can be switched between linear and logarithmic scales.
